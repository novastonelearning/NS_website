Good Morning Administrators,
As we discussed during our last administrators’ meeting at central office, the discipline
practices in our district must be addressed at the school level. Our most recent data summary
of district-wide disciplinary actions reveals a wide disparity between White and African
American students with rates of Out of School Suspension (OSS). This disparity is even more
glaring among White and African American males. A deeper dive into the data has revealed an
even more alarming situation: White and African American students, particularly males, are
receiving different disciplinary actions for the same or similar violations. As a district, we tend
to suspend (OSS) African American students more frequently than White students.
This is a trend that needs to be addressed at each school. Please take time with your
administrative and leadership team to explore the discipline data at your school and make
plans to address this disparity during your summer in-service activities.
We will plan to discuss this further at future administrator meetings.
MG
Michael Goodman, Ed.D.
Superintendent
Scottsboro Public Schools
