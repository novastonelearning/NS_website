About This Survey
The Educator Survey is a voluntary and confidential survey open to all certificated staff in the
school. The is anonymous and not evaluative but does provide feedback to administrators on
many important issues impacting the school.
Participation Rate
Survey results are only available if the survey sample includes ten or more people and the
response rate is greater or equal to 45 percent.
