FADE IN:
1 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, HALLWAY - DAY
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS, 34, appears to play the part of respected
leader except for the fact that she's nervously biting her
nails and checking her watch outside of a classroom.
She has red hair pulled back haphazardly with a clip and
wears a pantsuit reminiscent of Hillary Clinton.
The door to the classroom opens, she straightens her back and
puts on an err of calm and a smile.
Out steps EDDIE, 36, holding a pad of paper and a pen. He has
a kind demeanor despite his unwelcoming presence as a
representative from the school district.
EDDIE
You know you could have come in with
me, Principal Hastings.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
I've been told by some that my tactics
can be seen as micromanaging. It's
just my attempt to remove a little
pressure for my teachers when the
district is auditing their classes.
EDDIE
Unorthodox, but I like it. I can
always appreciate a supervisor who
listens to their employees.
Principal Hastings seems appreciative but mainly anxious.
EDDIE (CONT'D)
I can say now that things look fairly
good, but there is one thing I
observed today that I think we ought
to discuss privately.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
And I'm sure I know exactly what it
is. Let's head to my office and we can
talk further, Eddie.
2 INT. PRINCIPAL HASTING'S OFFICE - MOMENTS LATER
Principal Hastings and Eddie sit across from each other at
her desk. He gets settled and references his notes.

EDDIE
So you're already aware. Could you
explain it to me from your
perspective?
A deep breath… and she lets it all spill out.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
We're three weeks into the Nimble
Minds curriculum and though it's
required by the district, I still have
one teacher who is so staunch in her
refusal to use it that she allowed a
fidelity check to occur in her
classroom without even so much as
pretending to be using it. A little
frustrating if I had to put it
lightly.
Eddie sits surprised. As she catches her breath. He sees that
Hastings is burdened by this.
EDDIE
I understand that, overall, things are
going just as planned. The rest of
your teachers are on board and it
seems their instruction is on course.
So what exactly is the issue with her?
Hasting searches her mind for a possible response.
EDDIE (CONT'D)
I mean, Angela Simms is well known
within the district as a whole, not
just Morrison Elementary. We know her
to consistently have the highest
achievement scores in the building and
some of the highest in the district.
She's almost, dare I say, a model
educator.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Believe me. I know. She's won three
awards for highest achievement scores
in the district for her grade.
EDDIE
Four... but, point is she's got over
twenty years of experience and
achievement under her belt. I would
think she would know the consequences

of insubordination at this level, and,
frankly, I'm shocked.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Oh, I am too. It started with my
collaboration efforts.
Eddie looks confused.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS (CONT'D)
This is my third year here at
Morrison, and when the school year
started, one of my goals was to
increase collaboration among the
teams. They were used to working in
silos. I had to break them out of it.
EDDIE
I'm guessing it didn't go over well?
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
There was so much moaning and groaning
when I asked my faculty to simply
schedule some grade level team
planning... Required. I required it.
EDDIE
Sure. It's common to have your faculty
question a new method.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Right. The whole 'What we've been
doing works just fine. Why should we
have to change it?' ordeal.
Eddie appears to agree with the faculty on this one but tries
to empathize.
EDDIE
Okay, so based on the response to
required team planning, I can guess
that they weren't ecstatic to hear
that the district was requiring them
to adopt a new curriculum either?
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Not in the slightest. BUT I was
expecting it, so I was prepared with
reviews of the program. I showed them
the articles backing up what experts
have found drove student achievement

through the roof. But...
EDDIE
They've been there, done that?
Principal Hastings nods, smiles. He hit the nail on the head.
EDDIE (CONT'D)
I hope it helps to know that your
school is far from the only one to
show resistance. We've had pockets all
over the district, which is why we
made the statement letting faculty
know that failure to adopt the new
curriculum would result in a write up
on their profile.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
It's a little relieving to know it's
not just here. I feel a tad less
anxious now.
EDDIE
At this point we're several weeks in
and the issue seems to be mostly
resolved.
This a blow to Hastings' confidence.
EDDIE (CONT'D)
Have you gotten any feedback regarding
the resistance?
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
At first, it was a group of my more
senior teachers. They were quite clear
about it.
3 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, HALLWAY, FLASHBACK - DAY
Principal Hastings and VICE PRINCIPAL JAKES, 54, walk down
the hallway.
JAKES
I think we should be able to finish up
with prep by Friday.
Suddenly Hastings stops, she's reading something on her
phone. Jakes stops, a few paces ahead of her, curious.

JAKES (CONT'D)
What's wrong?
Hastings is startled, looks to him and then directly back
down to her phone. Whatever it is it has her full attention.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
I just got an email from a group of
teachers stating why they don't agree
with the new curriculum requirements.
(reading from her phone)
'Nimble Minds has a steep learning
curve. As many of the more tenured
teachers, we feel we have successfully
implemented strong and effective
curriculum in the past. We hope that
our opinion on the matter is well
taken into consideration.'
Jakes is taken aback. Hastings however, seems consumed.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS (CONT'D)
They're basically saying they don't
see the point in learning something
new because they're experienced in
this... consideration?!
Jakes tries to speak but is a little intimidated by how
worked up Hastings has reacted.
JAKES
There's no other option. It's required
by the district. They'll accept it
eventually and get on board. Just give
them a moment.
This seems to bring Hastings back to reality. She takes a
deep breath and presents with a smile.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Of course. You're right. It's not like
this is the first time a teacher or a
group of them has been opposed to a
new curriculum.
They continue walking down the hall.
4 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - DAY
Eddie listens intently to Hastings who is still seated across
from him.

PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
I thought it would end there.
(pause)
...and it did for the most part. All
except for Mrs. Simms.
EDDIE
Have you spoken with her privately
about it?
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
You bet I have. I'm in a difficult
spot with HER specifically. She's
super well liked by her colleagues in
the school, within the district, the
families she's interacted with,
parents and students alike. The
unofficial pillar of the Morrison
community. My goodness, alum come in
just to see her.
EDDIE
And how did that confrontation with
her go?
He’s poised to take notes.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
It didn't end the way I wanted but I
learned something I hadn't known
before.
5 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, CLASSROOM, FLASHBACK - DAY
Principal Hastings sits in a second grade classroom atop a
desk. Across from her, at her own desk, is ANGELA SIMMS, 57,
a class act with the gentility of a southern belle. Think
Barbara from Abbott Elementary.
ANGELA
I won't do it. Simply won't. There is
absolutely no reason why I can't
continue what I've been doing because,
you know it works just fine.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
I understand, but it's required by the
district now.
ANGELA
I know it is, and the district has

also acknowledged my abilities as an
educator. Multiple times.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Then you'll understand why it's
important to be a team player and
share your expertise with your grade
team. The district is enforcing it
across all the schools, not just
Morrison.
ANGELA
I've always planned as the year
progresses to make sure all of my
students are on board. Collaborating
with the team throws that off.
The BELL RINGS, ending the period.
ANGELA
(typing on her laptop)
I still have a stack of tests to grade
before the kids get back from lunch.
(to Hastings)
Respectfully, I'm done discussing
this. You won't change my mind.
6 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - DAY
EDDIE
(intrigued)
What seems to be the issue then? Write
her up. Boom, done, easy.
Principal Hastings holds her phone out to him, which he
takes.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Not quite.
POV at phone: It's a verified Twitter account with the handle
@AshleyAddresses, a picture of an early 30-something blonde-
haired mom as the thumbnail with a big, lip-sticked smile.
In the bio, it says "Follow my deep dive into pointless
progression and the ridiculous need for change for change
sake."
He scrolls down to see a Tweet that says:
"This week's discussion is with a local treasure at my

daughter's school, her teacher, Mrs. Simms! We'll be talking
about why our school is unnecessarily shifting to a new
learning curriculum. If her teaching style works, why change
it? Listen wherever you stream podcasts!"
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS (CONT'D)
Ashley Henson. Another parent emailed
me a screenshot of this tweet, so I
looked into Ashley's accounts. Each
social has at least twenty thousand
followers.
EDDIE
Ah.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Now, I could have a word with Ashley,
but seeing that she's basically an
influencer, that puts my career on the __
line.
EDDIE
...because you don't want to make her
more irritated on the matter than she
already presumably is.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Exactly, plus the other day...
7 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, FRONT OFFICE, FLASHBACK - MORNING
Ashley, wearing big sunglasses and carrying an expensive
purse, walks in with a small lunchbox. She removes the
sunglasses and places them on the top of her head.
WENDY, the permed secretary in her 50s takes the lunchbox.
ASHLEY
Thanks Wendy. I can't believe my
daughter walked out without her lunch
again.
WENDY
No worries. I'll make sure this gets
to her.
Ashley holds her phone upright as if she's recording.
ASHLEY
How much was it that was needed for
the field trip fundraiser?

WENDY
The second grade one to the zoo? Let
me double check, but I think it was
about three hundred dollars.
She leafs through some papers, and pulls out a spreadsheet.
WENDY (CONT'D)
Ah, yes. It's three hundred forty two
dollars.
Ashley rummages in her purse and pulls out her checkbook. PEN
SOUNDS of elaborate penmanship.
ASHLEY
Here's four fifty. Put whatever isn't
used in the lunch fund. I know that
one is always running low.
WENDY
Miss Henson, that's awfully generous
of you.
ASHLEY
I'm able to and I want to--I was one
of those kids, once upon a time.
Principal Hastings walks out from her office with a few
papers in hand as she texts on her phone.
WENDY
Oh, Principal Hastings, Miss Henson
topped off the second grade fundraiser
for their trip to the zoo and
contributed to the lunch fund.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
That's awesome! Thank you.
Ashley puts her sunglasses back on her face.
ASHLEY
Oh, don't mention it! Have a great
day, ladies!
Ashley exits.
8 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - DAY
Principal Hastings takes back her phone and pulls up TikTok.

PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
...so you can imagine my surprise when
I came across this on her TikTok. Not
only is she financially supporting the
school, but she documents it. And is
praised for it.
She pulls up another TikTok.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS (CONT'D)
This isn't the first time she's thrown
large sums of money towards our
expenditures. She gave us $1000
towards a new playground.
EDDIE
...and given that she's having Simms
on her podcast, you don't want to make
either of them upset because that puts
you in the dog house with media and
your career.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
I'm glad you understand where I'm
coming from. I've found myself stuck
between a rock and a hard place.
Because now...
9 INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, TEACHERS LOUNGE, FLASHBACK - DAY
Principal Hastings picks at some leftover noodles in a
tupperware container. A teacher enters. This is BRAD, 40s and
a seasoned educator.
BRAD
Hastings, got a minute?
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Yeah, what's on your mind?
He sits down across from her and takes a deep breath. He’s
nervous.
BRAD
Um... how do I put this?
Awkward pause.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
...are you okay?

BRAD
Yeah, yeah. I just don't want this to
come back to haunt me.
Hastings is skeptical, but safe.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
The room is Vegas. What happens here,
stays here. Nothing will be tied to
you in any way.
BRAD
Well... that's the thing. It can.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
10 Okay...
Another deep breath from Brad.
BRAD
Angela hasn't been participating in
our team planning like we've been
instructed... But I know you talked to
her last week about it. Is she getting
preferential treatment or something?
Hastings interrupts before he can say more.
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
She's not. I can't say anything more,
but I'm aware of it. Thanks for
bringing it to my attention, Brad.
INT. MORRISON ELEMENTARY, PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - DAY
Eddie looks at her with a contemplative look, skeptical.
EDDIE
Wow, okay. That's a doozy. So she's
blatantly ignoring requirements set
standard by the district, is being
endorsed by an influential parent by
means of a social platform, and, to
top it off, her teammates are noticing
her blatant ignorance?
PRINCIPAL HASTINGS
Right on the money. I've done
everything that I can. Is there
something that the district can help
me with?

Eddie takes a deep breath.
EDDIE
I wish there was an easy answer. But
we've given you all the guidance we
can. If Miss Simms is not cooperative
and you don't write her up, there's
nothing more we can do.
She sighs, knowing what difficult discussion lies ahead.
