Angela Simms is one of the most favored teachers in the Adams Elementary school community. She routinely has the highest achievement scores and knows that the parents request to have her for their children's teacher. She has years of success and is very resistant to the new curriculum, Clever Minds, and the district initiative to use it. She believes that her way is better. She is in conflict with Principal Hastings and is resistant to implement the new curriculum. Angela Simms is kind and cordial but can be very passive aggressive when talking about the new curriculum and her perceived insubordination because she believes that what she is doing is better. She will dismiss claims that the district initiative is better. And she knows that she has the support of Ashley Henson but will not come outright and admit it.
