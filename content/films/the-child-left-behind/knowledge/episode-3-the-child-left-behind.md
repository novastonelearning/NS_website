FADE IN:
1 INT. JOSH WILLIAMS' BEDROOM - MORNING
ANNA, 34, wears pajamas and a robe with a messy bun atop her
head. She searches frantically for something in a jumbled
teen boy's closet. She pulls out a red polo shirt and hold's
it up as her son, JOSH, 13 and detached, inspects.
JOSH
No.
ANNA
Josh. Come on. You can't miss the bus
today. It's just a shirt. Pick one.
JOSH
I'm not gonna miss the bus mom. I have
forty-five minutes.
ANNA
You have fifteen minutes.
Anna notices panic set into Josh's face as he stares at the
clock doing mental math. It's 5:55am. She tries to calm him.
ANNA
It's fine honey. It's early, don't be
hard on yourself.
(beat)
But come on. Hurry. We gotta go.
JOSH
I'm not going.
ANNA
What? Yes you are.
JOSH
There's no point. I'm an idiot. I
can't even figure out the time.
ANNA
It's not a big deal. Its early morning
and you got the time mixed up.
Everyone does it.
Tears well up in his eyes as he begins to unlace his shoes.

JOSH
(begging)
Mom. Please. I can't do this.
JOSH
Everyday I'm in trouble because I did
something wrong. My teachers are mad
at me, I can tell. It's so
embarrassing. I don't know what they
want. I can't do it.
Tears well up in Anna's eyes as she listens helplessly.
2 INT. CONFERENCE ROOM - MORNING
PRINCIPAL HARRIS, 43, has the kind, professional air of
Claire Huxtable. She and her colleagues work through a
problem near the end of their meeting.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
We can't afford it. I mean it is
definitely needed. The question is as
always, if funds are tight, where can
we pull from?
Behind the, grayed with tenure, Vice Principal, MR. CONWAY's
head is a powerpoint titled "2022 Q4 Budget."
MR. CONWAY
Well, my opinion is we have to forego
it. We've got a bigger issue on our
hands since the last quarter we've had
two additional students needing
paraprofessional support with their
I.E.P.'s.
Palpable stress and audible sighs fills the air.
BUILDING LEADER ONE
We've only got five parapros and
they're already stretched to the max.
MR. CONWAY
I know it isn't our favorite thing to
do but there's really only one thing
we can do...until next year.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
I wish there were another option but
there's not. Our hands are tied.

Mr. Conway accepts this easier than the others.
MR. CONWAY
Right. So we all agree? We'll have to
assess which students can afford a
reduction in time with their parapros
so it can be redistributed amongst the
newcomers?
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
(begrudgingly)
Just for the last quarter.
3 INT. SCHOOL'S FRONT OFFICE - MORNING
Anna signs Josh in. The admin assistant, JESS, 26 and bubbly
writes a note.
JESS
(to Josh)
Second period just started so you can
go ahead to class. Just give your
teacher this note.
Anna waits until Josh has left the room.
ANNA
Could I actually speak with the
principal?
JESS
Umm. Sure. She's just finishing a
meeting, if you'll have a seat.
ANNA
Thank you.
4 INT. PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - MORNING
Principal Harris opens the door and makes way for Mr. Conway
and the others to exit.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Much appreciated everyone.
Jess approaches Principal Harris and whispers something. She
nods her head and smiles as she spots a disheveled Anna
sitting zoned out in a chair across the room.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Mrs. Williams, would you like to join

me?
Anna grabs her things and takes a seat inside the office.
Principal Harris notices Anna's distress and kindly inquires.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
So what can I do for you Mrs.
Williams?
She takes a seat as Anna, a little relieved, begins.
ANNA
Well thank you for seeing me last
minute. Josh. My son. He's in the
seventh grade. He's been late a lot
recently and I wanted to talk to you
about it. He's having trouble. Long
story short we're going through this
routine where he freaks out and
doesn't want to come to school at all.
Then I have to soothe him enough to
get him here...
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
I see. We can speak with his first
period teacher and get him back on
track. Now when you say having
'trouble?' Anxiety? Because we have a
wonderful counselor...
Realizing she's not been clear, Anna gathers herself.
ANNA
Yes thank you. A chance to make up
work would be great.
Anna's distress grows. Principal Harris actively listens as
she turns to her computer to search for something.
ANNA
The anxiety. That's only part of it.
We work with him, got him a tutor.
He's doing well but recently it seems
like the process is more challenging
for him. He's frustrated and then I'm
frustrated...
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
I know that Josh deals with some
A.D.H.D. and dysgraphia but we
established an I.E.P. for this. It

seems to be helping. Despite what
you're describing he's still got a B
average. So you're doing something
right.
Anna cracks a smile in a brief moment of relief.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
So there's counseling. Or maybe a
little more time on exams?
ANNA
I did some research and I think he
could benefit from someone to support
him when he arrives and before he
leaves to help him transition
everyday. What I'm saying is that I'd
like to conduct a re-evaluation a year
early because I suspect another
disability. He needs more help.
Principal Harris is cornered between empathy and
helplessness. She returns to her computer screen.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
What exactly do you suspect?
ANNA
Well, I'm not sure exactly.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Mrs. Williams, I hope it helps to hear
that Josh seems to be doing fine. What
I'm seeing on the summary report tells
me that the other members of his
I.E.P. team don't see the same need
because his current services are
helping him reach his goals.
ANNA
What do they have to say about all of
his tardies? He's had four recently.
Principal Harris minimizes the gravity of the number.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Over the span of five weeks.
Offended by her writing it off, Anna holds it together.

ANNA
That's a pattern that I'm apparently
better at handling than I realized but
I have to be here. His current plan
isn't enough anymore.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
I can see that this troubling. How
about I reach out to his teachers?
Surely it would feel better to hear
from them how well he's doing.
ANNA
Are you saying 'no?'
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
The year is nearing a close, I say,
let's hear from Josh's teachers and
not make too hasty a decision.
Appearances can be deceiving.
Her suggestion isn't well received. Anna forces a smile.
ANNA
Thank you for your time.
She exits. Principal Harris returns to the budget paperwork
on her desk.
5 INT. TEACHER KITCHEN - DAY
Principal Harris on one of the empty desks of MRS. BAILEY,
53, who sits at her desk covered in papers.
MRS. BAILEY
I understand her worrying about his
emotional response, but personally,
I've not noticed Josh having any
particularly new difficulties. I mean
the coursework is increasing in
difficulty and frankly every student
experiences a little anxiety when that
happens. Josh is well behaved, seems
tuned in, his work hasn't raised any
red flags.
Principal Harris doesn't dig when she sees that Mrs. Bailey
is stumped.
MRS. BAILEY
But I'm only a part of his team. His

occupational therapist probably has
better insight because if he's having
problems he's masking it well.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
He's next on my list. Well I won't
take up any more of your time. But,
could you put that in writing for me
and email it over by end of day?
Principal Harris heads for the door and turns back.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Oh and Greta, sometimes parents just
need to see that there's genuine
attention given to their child. So if
you could pay close attention to him,
we'll explain to Mrs. Williams what
you've told me. I think it will help
tremendously.
MRS. BAILEY
Will do.
6 INT. PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - DAY
Principal Harris sits at her desk. She sends a text to Mr.
Conway that reads, "need to talk about one more budget issue
by end of day."
7 INT. ANNA'S KITCHEN - DAY
Anna stands at the counter listening through speaker phone.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS (O. S.)
I think that with the information laid
out in front of us, it is worth it to
try counseling for the anxiety before
putting Josh through another
evaluation.
Josh bursts through the front door looking a bit disheveled.
He removes his jacket and shoes. Anna, no longer focused on
the call, takes it off of speaker and puts it to her ear.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS (O.S.)
I would just hate to have the opposite
of the intended effect and disrupt his
progress.

ANNA
Okay. Thank you.
(to Josh)
How was the rest of your day?
JOSH
I have a book report due tomorrow so I
can't talk for the rest of the day.
He rushes off to his room. It weighs on Anna.
8 INT. JOSH'S ROOM - NIGHT
Josh wears his pajamas, book in hand, as he paces back and
forth reading a sentence repeatedly. He cannot grasp the
meaning and pulls at his hair in anger.
JOSH
(whispering)
"Madame Zeroni warned that if he
failed to do this, he and his
descendants would be doomed for all of
eternity."
A knock. He sets the book on the desk and opens the door.
ANNA
Hey. If you couldn't finish your
report, I don't want you to worry
about it. You just need to rest and
I'll write a note for you to give to
Mrs. Bailey tomorrow.
JOSH
It's okay. I'm done.
Anna's not sure but doesn't push. She runs her fingers
through his hair, kisses his head and walks away. Josh closes
the door, plops in bed returning to the same line again.
9 INT. ANNA WILLIAMS' KITCHEN - NIGHT
Anna sits staring at her phone, alone at the table, still in
distress as she taps away at the screen.
FADE TO BLACK
FADE IN

10 11 9.
INT. ANNA WILLIAMS KITCHEN - MOMENTS LATER
A Tik Tok video of Anna as we just saw her, now full screen.
ANNA
Hey guys. So as some of you know, I
have a thirteen year old who was
diagnosed with A.D.H.D. and
Dysgraphia. It's been two years since
he was evaluated. His I.E.P. was
helping, which for those of you who
don't know, stands for Individualized
Education Program. But recently he's
anxious and difficult and he's not
confident like he was.
Anna begins to tear up.
ANNA
It's so painful as a mother to watch
my son struggle. He's having to work
so much harder to achieve as much as
his peers. If I could I would just go
to school with him.
She laughs at herself and wipes her tears.
ANNA
But since that's not an option, I went
to his Principal today to request a
new evaluation. And do you know what
this woman told me? His Principal,
Mrs. Harris at Webb Middle School,
told me that...
The video continues on a loop, the sound fades out. Likes
appear on the heart icon. They rapidly increase to 132k

"@jaces_mom: They should be ashamed. #MommaBear"
"@kayla.cole86: The same thing happened to our daughter. It
can really hurt their confidence."
"@marksgotthespark: There are advocacy groups that can help
you navigate this. I'll DM you some. #ProtectStudents
#ListenToParents"
INT. SCHOOL'S FRONT OFFICE - MORNING
Principal Harris enters and smiles at her administrative

assistant.
12 PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Good morning Jess. How are you?
Jess plasters on an uncomfortable smile as she follows
Principal Harris into her office.
INT. PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS
She closes the door behind them and hands a stack of notes
over the desk to Principal Harris.
JESS
I've already gotten three calls.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
What is this?
JESS
It's the local news stations. They
want to talk to you about the video.
Principal Harris is visibly in the dark on the situation.
JESS
Oh you haven't seen it?
(beat)
Anna Williams, Josh's mom, made a
video about the school not doing
enough for Josh. It went viral over
night. Hundreds of thousands of views
and angry comments on the school's
Instagram page.
Principal Harris is flabbergasted and still confused.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
What? I just spoke with her yesterday.
We're still in the process.
JESS
Yea. Well I guess she's not happy with
that? The news channels want our
response. I told them I'd pass along
the message. Maybe we can just explain
that Josh...
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Thank you Jess. I appreciate that but
there are FERPA laws and district

communication policies in place that
restrict how we can publicly discuss a
student. I'm going to have to think on
this. The good news is we have a
school communication plan.
Mr. Conway pops out of his office as he hears Principal
Harris' voice.
MR. CONWAY
The good news is we haven't broken any
Special Education Laws. A PR problem
is a little easier to handle than a
legal one.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Not necessarily. This presents a
particularly touchy issue. It would be
easy to say these are the particular
points we would like to refute but
then there's the subject of Josh. It's
hard to refute the points without
discussing him specifically.
Principal Harris takes all of this in.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Jess, please send a message over the
speaker before the students arrive.
Tell all faculty and staff to look out
for an email that will be coming from
me shortly.
MR. CONWAY
Yes. Remind everyone of expectations
around talking about this. It's moving
quickly.
PRINCIPAL HARRIS
Let me start by watching the video and
then we can come up with a plan.
FADE OUT
