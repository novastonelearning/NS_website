Dr. Harris,
I have been auditing school service and staffing levels since our September district principal
meeting where I shared the update on the special education funding levels in the district.
According to the number of service hours on the IEPs from the children in your school, you
appear to be staffed at an appropriate level. If you feel like you need more staff to meet the needs
of the paraprofessional hours in the IEPs of the children you serve, then I suggest your team take
some time to map the hours required (from the IEPs) and the available staff in the school and
match that to the master schedule of the building. You will likely find areas where you can
double up support time or reschedule courses to make staff available for paraprofessional support
time. You need to make your current staffing level work, and I suggest that you carefully
consider any new requests for paraprofessional support in your upcoming IEP meetings and
ensure that you have data to support such a request in the IEP. Your request at this time for
additional personnel or funding is denied.
Donna Wright, Ph.D.
Executive Director for Special Education
Maryville County Public Schools
