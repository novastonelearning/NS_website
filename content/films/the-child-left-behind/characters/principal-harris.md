Principal Harris is kind but can also bristle if she feels that she is being unfairly challenged. She feels enormous pressure from the district to be mindful of the school budget and allocated resources while also meeting the needs of all of her students in the school. She is tries to run a tight ship in the school and sometimes this can lead to conflict because she may be unaware of deeper perspectives of situations such as with the conflict with Anna Willams. Principal Harris is willing to admit when she is wrong and wants to do the right thing for her school.
