Anecdotal Information and Facts Surrounding the Case
The school consists of, mostly, young and inexperienced faculty. Many of the faculty in the
school have been teaching for five years or less, while only a handful of teachers have 15 or
more years of experience. The school has a high population of English Learners, which makes
having the EL license endorsement almost a necessity for employment in the building. There are
a total of 18 classroom teachers in the school: one Prek teacher, two Special Education teachers,
and three teachers for each grade level (K-4). You must choose two non-renewals from your
non-tenured faculty, which means the teachers selected will not be automatically placed
somewhere else and will need to search and interview for a new position at a school that is
hiring. Listed below are the nine teachers eligible for displacement and descriptors for each
teacher. The Level of Effectiveness (LOE) score is the summative evaluation score for the year
and is reported on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being significantly below expectations, 2 at expectations,
and 5 significantly above expectations.

a. EL certified
b. 3rd year teaching
c. Teach for America alum
d. Christy is an energetic teacher who is very passionate about her students. She has
a laser focus on equity for all students.
e. LOE score: 4.2
