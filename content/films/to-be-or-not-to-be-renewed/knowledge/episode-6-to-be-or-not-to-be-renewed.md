EARN A LICENSE ENDORSEMENT TO TEACH ELL STUDENTS!
He picks up the phone and dials. The phone on speaker...
EVAN

JULIE

EVAN

JULIE

the research already. There is a local university oEering a ELL certification program.
EVAN
Yes. I know.
JULIE
Great, well what I wanted to tell you is that we’ve been allocated two seats in this
endorsement with the cohort.

EVAN
Oh. I actually didn’t know that. Thank you Julie. That’s very helpful. I appreciate it.
Evan leans back in his chair and takes a sigh of release. As he contemplates what this
means he seems to be weighed down again rather than relieved.
INT. MAIN OFFICE - AFTERNOON

EL CERTIFIED. 3RD YEAR TEACHING. TEACH FOR AMERICA ALUM. HAS A LOT OF

As the question and answer portion of the meeting takes place, the faces of the teachers

EVAN

JOHN
