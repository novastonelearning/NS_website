Ms. Joy Reid is a 33-year-old teacher at Longhunter Elementary who is overly-confident, disingenuous, and "always ready to complain," serving as the head of the Faculty Sunshine Committee while consistently resisting every change Principal Flemming has attempted to implement since his arrival three years ago. She is motivated by a desire to maintain the comfortable, laid-back environment that existed under Principal Allen's leadership, and she openly expresses nostalgia for a time when it was "really nice not being micromanaged." Reid's temperament is passive-aggressive and dismissive—she makes sarcastic comments about Flemming's policies, complains about having to monitor students "twenty-four/seven," and shows little regard for the fiscal procedures that Flemming has worked hard to establish. She reacts to the new audit requirements with defiance and carelessness, as evidenced by the audit report's findings that she held collections for extended periods ranging from 8 to 44 days before remitting them to the bookkeeper, with one Faculty Sunshine collection of $255 held for forty-four days after the initial collection date—a clear and egregious violation of the three-day deposit policy that all staff were trained on during July in-service and again after winter break. Reid signed the Fiscal Policy Acknowledgement Form stating "I understand that failure to follow these fiscal rules will result in disciplinary action," yet her repeated violations demonstrate either willful disregard for the rules or incompetence in managing the significant sums of money entrusted to her as committee chair. Her resistance to Flemming's leadership and her pattern of undermining his authority make her audit violations particularly problematic, as they represent not just a technical infraction but a broader challenge to his efforts to bring accountability and professionalism to the school.
