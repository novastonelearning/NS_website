Principal Mark Flemming is a 46-year-old "vanilla, rule-abiding man" and "cheery little cornball" who leads Longhunter Elementary School (also called Sunshine Elementary in the script) with an unwavering commitment to raising standards and achieving a clean audit report, even if it means sacrificing popularity. He is motivated by a deep belief that the school needed important and necessary changes after years of lax fiscal management under his beloved predecessor, Mr. Allen, and he takes pride in being thorough, prepared, and policy-driven rather than focused on being liked. Flemming's temperament is relentlessly positive and optimistic—he insists he and Mr. Allen are "neck and neck on the likability scale" despite clear evidence that the community still mourns Allen's easygoing leadership—but beneath his cheerful exterior lies frustration and exhaustion from three years of resistance to every change he's tried to implement. He reacts to challenges by doubling down on rules and procedures, conducting multiple trainings on fiscal policy, requiring signed acknowledgment forms from all staff, and reminding everyone that "handling money is serious business" with consequences for infractions. However, when the long-awaited audit report arrives revealing violations by both Ms. Joy Reid—a resistant teacher who held collections for 8 to 44 days and has undermined his leadership at every turn—and Mrs. Mary Johnson—a beloved three-time Teacher of the Year who held less than $10 for just a few days and recently offered to be his champion in the building—Flemming finds himself in an agonizing dilemma. The district policy leaves the consequences entirely to his discretion, from "a slap on the wrist to relieving her of her position," and he knows that whatever decision he makes will define his credibility as a leader: treat both violations uniformly and risk losing the one teacher who could finally help him win over the staff, or apply nuanced judgment and face accusations of favoritism and hypocrisy after promising strict enforcement with "no exceptions."
