Principals,
I am emailing today regarding the new district literacy curriculum, Clever Minds. During
conversations and school visits, I am being made aware of a level of resistance to this new
curriculum. It has been seven years since we adopted new literacy curriculum in the district, and
we are most certainly overdue for a change as our literacy scores have been stagnant in recent
years. Change is hard - I realize that – but this change to Clever Minds is a non-negotiable. We
will proceed with full implementation of this curriculum and expect to see it used with fidelity
across all classrooms. As a reminder from our recent principals’ meeting:
1. 2. 3. The Board approved adoption of this curriculum and district policy states that approved
curriculum is the only curriculum to be used in schools.
Our literacy scores dictate that inaction is unacceptable. This shift in curriculum is the
action we are taking to make a difference across the district.
Teachers who do not implement the curriculum will face disciplinary action including, up
to, termination.
This is the path forward in our district. I expect you all to be positive spokespeople for this
change to help with implementation.
Clayton
Clayton Barden, Ed.D.
