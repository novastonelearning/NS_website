Dr. Donna Summers is the principal of East Fork Elementary School, a professional and well-dressed leader who walks with certainty but finds herself trapped in an impossible situation. She is motivated by a deep commitment to her students, her teachers, and maintaining the high achievement record her school has built over the years, and she genuinely believes in allowing educators the autonomy and creativity that once made her and Clayton Barden successful as classroom teachers. Donna's temperament is diplomatic and measured—she seeks to address concerns through conversation, clarification, and transparency, hoping that misunderstandings about Clever Minds can be resolved peacefully. However, she is increasingly exhausted and defeated as she faces relentless pressure from all sides: teachers who feel the curriculum is restrictive and inappropriate, parents who accuse the program of indoctrination and demand its removal, and district leadership that insists on strict compliance with no room for flexibility. She reacts to conflict by trying to maintain calm and professionalism, even when confronted with angry outbursts from staff and hostile accusations at town hall meetings, but the mounting stress takes a visible toll. Donna feels trapped, knowing that every decision she makes will alienate someone, and despite her best efforts to be patient, transparent, and solution-oriented, she realizes she is in a no-win situation where no response will satisfy everyone.
