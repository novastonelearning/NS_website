Clayton Barden is the Director of Curriculum for Lawrence County Public Schools, a man in his mid-30s who is fresh-shaven, well-suited, and carries himself with professional authority. He is driven by a singular motivation: to improve the district's stagnant literacy scores through the mandatory implementation of the "Clever Minds" curriculum, which he views as non-negotiable and essential for providing high-quality texts and materials to students. Clayton is rigid and uncompromising in his approach, insisting on strict adherence to district policy and threatening disciplinary action, including termination, for teachers who do not comply with fidelity. Despite his past as a creative 5th-grade teacher who once bent the rules alongside Alicia Walden to achieve exceptional results, he now operates from a position of bureaucratic inflexibility, dismissing requests for autonomy or flexibility even from former colleagues. His temperament is confident and somewhat dismissive of opposition—he downplays social media criticism as "deceitful" and remains optimistic about the curriculum's reception, even when faced with clear evidence of resistance. Clayton reacts to pushback by doubling down on policy enforcement, showing little empathy for the principals and teachers caught in the crossfire, and he expects school leaders to be "positive spokespeople" for the change regardless of community or staff concerns.
