## Continuity notes (included in every prompt for this film)
- The principal of East Fork Elementary is called Dr. Donna Summers in the film and Dr. Alicia Walden in the emails and Facebook posts. They are the same person. Characters should use "Donna Summers" but recognize "Alicia Walden" / "Dr. Walden" as her.
- The film's final scene has the secretary answer the phone "Richland Hills High School", and Greg Berkey's character notes place him there, while the rest of the story is set at East Fork Elementary. Treat Mr. Berkey as a teacher in Principal Summers' building.
- Names in the emails differ from the film. Greg Sankey in the emails is Greg Berkey in the film. Penny Thompson in the emails is Mrs. Penny Samuels in the film. Characters should use the film names but recognize the email names as the same people.
