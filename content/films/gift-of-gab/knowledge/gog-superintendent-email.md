Principals,
Thank you for a great end to another great school year. I am very optimistic going into this
summer break that we will build on the strong work of this past year. As I mentioned in our last
principals’ meeting, one of the “building blocks” will be greater instructional rigor and
accountability. We previously discussed placing a focus on technology and 21st century skills in
our instructional planning and delivery in the next academic year. This should be a priority in
your schools next year and technology use should carry a lot of weight when you do classroom
observations for teacher evaluations. Please refer to the districts strategic plan for the teacher
evaluation and expectations regarding technology use in the classroom. I strongly encourage
you to make this a priority as you make plans for professional development this school year.
JA
Justin Andrews, Ph.D.
Superintendent
Southland Public Schools
