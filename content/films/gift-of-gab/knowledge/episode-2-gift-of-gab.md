Gift of Gab
Revision 2
Austin Mitchell
Revisions by
Jack Pearl

0A INT. HALLWAYS - DAY
PRINCIPAL HAYNES(40s), a jovial woman wearing bright colors
and khaki pants, walks down the hall with a teacher beside
him.
MRS. HOUSE (50s, black), a woman whose disarming smile
disguises the disciplinarian within, carries a mailer's box
full of knick-knacks and office supplies.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Thanks for coming in, Mrs. House. I
know it's early, but I want to make
sure all of you try out our new Smart
Sound system before the year starts.
MRS. HOUSE
Smart Sound?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
A new tech suite the district is
implementing. It's supposed to provide
students with a better learning
experience. Complete with smartboards,
surround sound speakers in the
classroom, even 360 cameras so you can
record your lessons.
MRS. HOUSE
Wow, fancy. Never had anything like
this when I was in the city.
Principal Haynes and Mrs. House arrive at a classroom. He
holds his arm out as if to say "after you".
INT. MRS. HOUSE'S CLASSROOM - DAY - CONTINUOUS
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Should make teachers' lives easier.
The SCREECH of a LOUDSPEAKER FEEDING BACK cuts her off as
Mrs. House places her box down on her desk.
MRS. HOUSE
(smirking)
It'll make our lives louder, at least.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
It'll be fine. Play around with it a

little and test it out. We'll meet
back in Mr. Miller's room after lunch
to see where everyone's at. User's
manual is on your desk. Let me know if
you need anything!
Principal Haynes charismatically wraps her knuckles on the
classroom door as he disappears down the hallway.
The LOUDSPEAKERS SCREECH again as Mrs. House grazes her hand
past a stray cable dangling off her desk.
MRS. HOUSE
Some ear plugs would be nice.
1 INT. AROUND TEACHERS' CLASSROOMS - MONTAGE - DAY
TEACHER #1 swipes a smart marker on the smart board, only for
the mark to be offset a foot to the right of where they tried
to write.
MR. MILLER (50s, white), an athletic man whose sports polo
and cargo shorts scream football coach, stares at a control
panel hooked up to his computer that says "SYNCING." The
machine pinwheels in an endless cycle of buffering.
Mrs. House speaks into the microphone on her desk, only to
receive SCREECHING FEEDBACK from the SPEAKERS in all corners
of her classroom. She covers her ears.
TEACHER #2 uses the control panel to adjust the 360 camera.
It points straight into the ceiling and freezes in place.
Mr. Miller continues to stare at the control panel screen,
which still says "SYNCING." Suddenly, the control panel says
"GOODBYE" and shuts off. Mr. Miller SLAMS his FIST on the
table, leans back in his seat, and clutches his head.
Mrs. House, still covering her ears, marches over to her desk
and RIPS out the CABLES powering the system. The SCREECHING
stops. She sits down and sighs. Suddenly, the same SCREECHING
continues in another classroom overhead...
2 INT. MR. MILLER'S CLASSROOM - LATER.
Principal Haynes stands at the front with 10 teachers
scattered throughout. Among them are Mrs. House and Mr.
Miller.
The whiteboard behind Principal Haynes has a big count down
to the new school year written on it. Ten days left.

PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Thanks for coming in today, gang. I
know everyone here was just to dying
come back to school and spend your
last few days of freedom with me.
A murmur of polite laughter cuts the obvious tension.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
I just wanted to know how everyone was
coming along with our new
enhancements.
MR. MILLER
Not at all.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
They did say there might be a bit of a
learning curve.
MR. MILLER
Learning cliff, more like it.
A genuine chorus of laughter bursts through the room.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Alright, alright. What's going on?
MR. MILLER
What's going on? Half the time the
system won't actually turn on, it just
freezes and says that it's "syncing."
Otherwise it's shrieking like a stuck
pig.
MRS. HOUSE
I unplugged it in Ms. Johnson's room.
It was giving me a migraine from the
second floor.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Alright, I'll call the tech.
MR. MILLER
We've already tried that.
MRS. HOUSE
The poor kid's been running around all
morning. I don't think he's gotten a
single one to work.

PRINCIPAL HAYNES
OK, I'll call the rep. Maybe there's
something that he can do. But we gotta
make it work, guys. The district is
expecting a big bump in test scores
from this--
MR. MILLER
(muttering to others)
By "test scores," they mean "teachers'
migraines.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
And how well you use the suite is
going to be on your evaluations for
merit bonuses.
MRS. HOUSE
What?!
The room breaks out in angry protestation.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
All the more reason to get on board.
In the meantime, keep trying to figure
it out.
(to Mrs. House)
And don't unplug the thing.
3 INT. PRINCIPAL HAYNES' OFFICE - THE NEXT DAY
Principal Haynes escorts MR. CONNER (50s, white) into her
office. Wearing a t-shirt, shorts, and flip flops, she looks
underdressed next to Mr. Conner's department store suit.
Techs buzz in and out of the classrooms and in the hallway
behind them. POWER TOOLS ROAR in the background.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
I appreciate you coming personally to
oversee this, Mr. Conner, but I have
to admit it feels a bit unnecessary.
They take a seat at Principal Haynes' desk.
MR. CONNER
I understand Principal Haynes, but
when I saw your concerns I knew that I
was needed on site. I don't like doing
business at arms' length, you see.
Leaves too much room for mistakes.

PRINCIPAL HAYNES
That's good to hear. The staff's been
on edge since they found out how much
the district has invested in this.
MR. CONNER
They've got nothing to worry about.
Just you wait. There's always an
adjustment period, but before fall
break I personally guarantee that your
faculty won't ever want to teach in a
school without this suite.
Principal Haynes leans back in her chair and takes a sip of
coffee from her "World's Best Principal" mug.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
I suppose you've danced this dance
before.
MR. CONNER
(chuckling)
Once or twice. Teachers are like
anyone: they hate change. Once they
see how much better things are with it
though, they'll be dying for the next
best thing.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Like a kid with a new toy.
MR. CONNER
Exactly. I expect we'll be back here
next year installing the upgrades.
Principal Haynes sets her coffee mug down and leans forward
in his chair.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Upgrades?
MR. CONNER
Of course! There's so much more we can
offer. We're talking dynamic lesson
planning with integration to our
library of teaching materials,
partnerships with educational content
creators, virtual field trips. The
works!

PRINCIPAL HAYNES
That all sounds great, but I think we
have our hands full with this for now.
MR. CONNER
Of course, of course! Forgive me, I
can get a bit excited.
(beat)
Y'know, I've found that the best way
to learn our technology is to see how
an expert uses it.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
I'm sure, but we're the first ones in
the county to use this, and frankly I
think all the neighboring counties are
using us as a litmus test.
MR. CONNER
And rightly so, can't go putting all
your eggs in a brand new basket.
Luckily I know the perfect school.
(beat)
Rob Clark Academy!
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Rob Clark uses this?
MR. CONNER
Has since day one! Rob's an old friend
of mine and was an early adopter. His
faculty works closely with us anytime
we have an update in development. I
bet your faculty would be old hands
after a walk through with him and his
staff.
Principal Haynes looks at the clock on the wall.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Tell me about this Rob Clark thing.
4 INT. CLASSROOM - THE NEXT DAY
The town hall has regathered, this time the knives are out
without pretense. Everyone glares at Principal Haynes.
The whiteboard calendar says eight days.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
I've seen your emails--

MR. MILLER
So the computers still work at least.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
And while I understand your
frustrations, I want to remind you
that this suite is for our students'
benefit. Even if you don't care about
the merit pay, I know you care about
our kids' development, so--
MR. MILLER
How is this good for their
development? By giving them live tech
support training?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Frank, let me finish.
Mr. Miller shares a look with Mrs. House.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
So the rep told me that there's a
hands-on training tutorial--
MR. MILLER
The techs here have been useless!
What's a tutorial going to change?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
It's not just any tutorial. It's with
Rob Clark.
MRS. HOUSE
Rob Clark?
The angry mob has dissolved. The teachers now whisper amongst
themselves in optimistic excitement.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Yes! Turns out Smart Sound includes an
all-inclusive tutorial package with
Rob Clark and his staff with the
system's installation.
MRS. HOUSE
Almost seems too good to be true. How
are we just now finding out about it?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
You know how dry those contracts can

be. The school board probably just
missed it when they signed the deal
for the suite. Anyway, the good news
is Smart Sound will be covering all
expenses: food, lodging, the
conference, all of it. And by the end
of it, we should all be experts.
MR. MILLER
When is this tutorial?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Friday through Sunday. If all goes
well, I'll give you the rest of the
time off until school starts. I'm
working with Gladys to get
transportation and lodging set up.
Sound good?
The crowd of teachers nod their head in agreement and begin
to disperse. Mrs. House crosses her arms and leans back in a
chair, sharing a glance with Mr. Miller.
5 INT. PRINCIPAL HAYNES' OFFICE - THAT NIGHT
Principal Haynes sits in her office. The strain of the new
year has already sent it into complete disarray.
The PHONE RINGS, startling her from her spreadsheet-fueled
malaise. She picks it up and puts it on speaker.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Hello?
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
Principal Haynes, I apologize for
calling you at this late hour but
didn't want to leave this on the back
burner.
Principal Haynes stands up, stretches, and begins to pace
around her office.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
How can I help you?
MR. CONNER I (V.O.)
It's nothing serious. Just a little
issue with the bean counters.

PRINCIPAL HAYNES
What do you mean?
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
Well, because your district opted out
of the tutorial initially, Rob Clark
is giving us a whole mess of red tape.
Principal Haynes stops her pacing and leans over the phone on
her desk.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Opted out? No, no, I just got my
faculty on board with this.
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
No need to worry. I'll get it sorted
out. You have my word.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Well, thanks for the call. I
appreciate the heads up.
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
Of course, of course. Oh, by the way,
Rob Clark usually gives people a
chance to talk about how the system is
improving their schools. What do you
say, mind putting in a good word for
us? Might convince your district to
implement our system across the board.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Uh, sure. That's not a problem.
MR. CONNER
Excellent! I look forward to seeing
you this weekend.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Likewise.
Principal Haynes hangs up the phone rubbing her temples.
6 INT. ADMINISTRATION OFFICE LOBBY - DAY
Principal Haynes strolls down the hallway with a yawn. As she
approaches the administration office, she hears a cacophony
of outrage. Confused, he swings the door open into...

6A INT. ADMINISTRATION OFFICE - CONFERENCE ROOM - CONTINUOUS
Mrs. House and Mr. Miller bicker loudly. Gladys waves her
arms to calm the storm, but to no avail.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Hey, hey! What's going on here?
MRS. HOUSE
What do you actually know about this
tutorial package?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Well, uh, I know Mr. Connor raves
about it. He did call me last night
saying there might be a paperwork
issue, but--
MRS. HOUSE
It doesn't exist!
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
What?
MRS. HOUSE
I knew something seemed fishy, so I
called a friend of mine on the board
to take a closer look into this Smart
Sound contract. Turns out there's
nothing about some fancy, all-
expenses-paid tutorial.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
That can't be right, Mr. Conner said
the district opted out of the
tutorial.
MR. MILLER
If the district wanted us to use this
stupid system so badly, why would they
opt out of something that would help
us?
MRS. HOUSE
Right? It doesn't make any sense!
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
This is just a big misunderstanding.
Mr. Conner said his secretary would
call our office and sort this out.
Isn't that right, Gladys?

Gladys shifts her eyes away from the teachers and sheepishly
makes eye contact with Principal Haynes.
GLADYS
Well, that's the thing. They did call.
I asked them about the invoice we sent
them for the transportation and
lodging expenses. Turns out they're
not covering it at all. In fact,
they're not covering the tutorial
either...
MR. MILLER
What?!
MRS. HOUSE
How much does the tutorial cost?
GLADYS
Four hundred per person. Forty-four
hundred total, just for the walk
through.
Mrs. House and Mr. Miller break out into even louder chaos.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Hang on!
(to Gladys)
Can't we just earmark this like we do
for field trips? Pull from our normal
funds?
GLADYS
We could, but those things take time.
Earliest I could get these approved
would be Monday.
MRS. HOUSE
So, what? Are we expected to pay for
this trip out of pocket? With our
salaries, I know I won't be paying a
dime for this.
MR. MILLER
That's it! It was gonna ruin a good
fishing trip anyway. Screw this!
Mrs. House and Mr. Miller begin to exit the office. Principal
Haynes rubs her palms against his forehead before regaining
his composure.

PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Wait! Just because there's a
misunderstanding about the tutorial
doesn't mean we're giving up on the
system. Remember, it's for the kids!
MRS. HOUSE
Do you honestly think this Mr. Conner
cares about our kids?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
He wouldn't lie about something like
this.
MRS. HOUSE
He wouldn't? The man's not an
educator, Marissa! He's a salesman
trying to make a quick buck and bleed
us dry. I wish you would see that...
The teachers continue out of the office when...
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
Alright, fine! I'm going to call him
and figure this out, O.K.
Principal Haynes marches over to the conference table and
grabs the phone to dial Mr. Conner. Mrs. House and Mr. Miller
watch with irate expressions.
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
Principal Haynes! Excited for the
trip, my friend?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
When were you planning on telling me
the tutorial was never included? Nice
way to "take care of us." Just sneak a
fast one by us and have us foot the
bill, huh?
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
Woah, hang on. I told you, it was an
accounting error. We promise to fully
reimburse you once the paperwork is
sorted out, I swear.
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
We don't have that kind of money just
laying around!
(beat)

Look, I appreciate your help so far,
but we will not be going to the
tutorial after all.
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
Wait, wait, wait. Principal Haynes,
I'm not trying to take advantage of
this situation. I understand your
frustrations. So, as an act of good
faith, I'll write you and your faculty
a personal check covering all costs
for the weekend. How's that sound?
PRINCIPAL HAYNES
That's... very generous of you. What's
the catch?
MR. CONNER (V.O.)
No catch. I like you, and I know your
faculty really needs our help. So,
what do you say?
Principal Haynes looks at Mrs. House, Mr. Miller, and Gladys
who stare back at her to see her next move.
Should Principal Haynes accept the money from Mr. Conner and
go on the trip? Can Mr. Conner be trusted? How should you
approach the district about the Smart Sound representative
and the issues with the system itself? How would you defuse
the situation with your teachers?
7A OMIT
