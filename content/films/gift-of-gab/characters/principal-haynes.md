Principal Haynes's school has been selected for the new Smart Sounds technology suite. She is feeling the pressure from the district to increase student achievement, improve teacher morale, and to implement the new, expensive technology with fidelity. She can be a bit of a pushover and hesitant to take a stand because she doesn't want to upset anyone's feelings, but she knows what the school needs and knows that it has some work to do. She will take a stand for what is right if pushed hard enough. She is a bit unsure about the way that the CEO of Smart Sounds, Mr. Connor, is handling the business deal with the technology and feels a bit uneasy about the business arrangement for the tutorial trip with Smart Sounds. She trusts the district contract process, but feels uneasy because of the district policies on gifts and vendors. She wants to do the right thing but also does not want to upset anyone.
