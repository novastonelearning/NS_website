Corey Allen
Finding a New Path Forward
College Entrance Essay
Saint Duke’s University
Throughout my life and into my teenage years, I would look around at my peers and wonder
how they had so much while I had so little. It always seemed like everything came harder to
me or I had to put in extra eBort just to make things work. My father left my mother and I
when I was just five years old. I do not know where he went or much about why he left our
family. I suppose my mother always wanted to shield that from me and protect me from the
truth. My father leaving us always left a void in my heart that I have tried every day to fill with
goodness and love for my mother and for others with whom I would come in contact.
As I mentioned, life was not easy for me and my mother. I remember her always working
two jobs just to make ends meet. I know it was hard for her and that the long hours for little
pay made some months really tight around our home. Although I knew we didn’t have a lot,
she always seemed to find a way to make something work for me. New basketball shoes for
the upcoming season? She would find a way to buy them for me. An upcoming church
camp trip? She would find the money to make it happen. She was and is my hero and
provided for me when my father never would.
No one in my family has ever been to college. My mother, grandparents, and other family
members are all hard-working individuals who earn an honest living. College was
something that was always out of reach, primarily because of the financial commitment
that was required to attend college. I have earned straight A’s since I was in middle school,
earned a 32 on the ACT, support my church and community, and recently was selected to
serve on my school’s student advisory board for school safety. While receiving the trustee
scholarship and earning acceptance to Saint Duke’s University would be such a great
honor, it would more importantly be a life changing event that would put my family’s
generational trajectory on a diBerent path.
