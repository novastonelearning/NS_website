Mason Gill is a White upperclassman and Eagle Scout at Mariana Adams High School who has been selected to serve on Principal Ford's newly created Student Safety Council, a position that reflects his reputation as a tremendous leader and outstanding student. He is motivated by a genuine desire to help keep his school safe, though he's also aware of the social cost of being perceived as a "snitch" by his peers, joking with fellow council members about becoming "the billboard for snitches." Mason's temperament is earnest and responsible—he takes his commitments seriously, as evidenced by his achievement of Eagle Scout status and his willingness to immediately report to Principal Ford when he realizes he has a pocket knife in his possession after forgetting to remove it from his jacket following a weekend scouting activity. He reacts to discovering the violation with nervous anxiety, clearly fearful of the consequences but compelled by his sense of duty and honesty to come forward. Mason has a close personal connection to Principal Ford that extends beyond school: his family attends the same church as Ford, invited him to Mason's Eagle Scout ceremony, and gave Ford a generous gift card to his favorite clothing store as a token of appreciation for Ford's support over the years. This relationship places Mason in a unique position when he finds himself sitting in the front office alongside Torian Jackson—a student with a lengthy disciplinary history—waiting to face consequences under the same zero tolerance policy that Ford has publicly promised will be applied with no exceptions.
