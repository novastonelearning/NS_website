Principal Brian Ford is the leader of Mariana Adams High School (also called Southgate H.S. in the script), a man who projects matter-of-fact confidence and firm resolve as he faces mounting pressure from parents, the community, and Superintendent Dr. Justin Simpson to address a 46% surge in student disciplinary referrals for fighting, drug possession, and theft. He is motivated by a genuine desire to protect students from the dangers of violence and drugs like fentanyl and ecstasy, and he believes that strict, by-the-book enforcement through a "zero tolerance policy" with swift and stern consequences—including expulsion and criminal charges—is the only way to "clean up the school's act." Ford's temperament is authoritative and decisive in public settings, confidently laying out harsh penalties at parent meetings and implementing random drug sweeps with School Resource Officer Frankie Green, but he also shows a softer, more personal side in his relationships with students like Cory Allen, whom he treats "like one of his own" and mentors closely. He reacts to the escalating crisis by creating a Student Safety Council of five exemplary student leaders to serve as policy advisors and positive role models, though this decision becomes complicated by his personal connections to some council members, including Cory (whom he's helping get into college) and Mason Gill (whose family attends his church and gave him a generous gift card). When faced with multiple simultaneous violations—Torian's marijuana possession, Mason's pocket knife, and Cory's marijuana falling from his backpack—Ford finds himself in an impossible position where his zero tolerance policy collides with his personal relationships, forcing him to confront whether he can apply the rules equally or if his connections will influence his decisions.
