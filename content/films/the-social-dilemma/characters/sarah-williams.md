Sarah Williams is a second-grade teacher at Pikeville Elementary School who is widely celebrated as "an amazing person and teacher" and a "foundational member of this school community," with the highest test scores in the school, two Teacher of the Year awards, constant involvement in PTO events, and a reputation for staying late to tutor struggling students. She is motivated by a genuine dedication to her students and school community, as evidenced by her willingness to come in during summer break and put in long hours to help make the PTO July 4th celebration event a success, which parents describe as "just a blessing to the school and all the kids and families." Sarah's temperament appears outwardly positive and committed—parents routinely tell the PTO president that she is "a wonderful person" who "makes them smile every day," and Principal Sandrell describes her as "a special one" whom the school would "hang on to at all costs." However, beneath this exemplary exterior, Sarah harbors deep frustrations with district leadership and policies that she expresses through an anonymous Twitter account (@anonymousdistrict), where she posts scathing criticisms about administrative raises while teachers get nothing, new restorative justice policies, teacher exodus, declining district quality, mandatory benchmark assessments, and scripted curriculum that takes away "teaching time and academic freedom." She reacts to district decisions with anger and cynicism, using phrases like "trash district," "train wreck," and "terminate them all," and when Principal Reeves nearly collides with her in the hallway while Sarah is distracted by her phone, the encounter leaves Reeves with an unsettling feeling that ultimately leads to the discovery that Sarah is behind the anonymous account that has been causing chaos throughout the district.
