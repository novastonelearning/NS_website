Social Dilemma
Revision 2
Kariss Forte
COE PROJECT - EPISODE 8
2023 kariss.forte@gmail.com

FADE IN
1 INT. FREEMONT ELEMENTARY, FRONT OFFICE - MORNING
PRINCIPAL REEVES, a 39 year old woman looks on helplessly as
she tries to put together what exactly is happening on the
phone call in front of her.
She stands in front of BEN, 35, at his desk and listens
nervously while trying to interject with the person on the
other end of the phone.
BEN
Yes. I've already spoken with
Principal Reeves. We have no comment
at this time.
Principal Reeves gives him a thumbs up as Ben tries to get
another word in.
BEN (CONT'D)
No comment. No comment...Thank you.
He hangs up the phone. A sigh of relief.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Who was it this time?
BEN
Fox. It's the anonymous Twitter
account. You saw last night's series
of tweets, right?
PRINCIPAL REEVES
But of course. Well good job handling
that.
BEN
I just hope we can figure out who's
running the account and get it taken
down. They obviously have inside info
on the district. I wish I could make
THEM answer the phone.
They share a short lived chuckle.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
With my luck it's someone in this
building.
Ben shoots her a sympathetic expression as she walks off.

2 INT. FREEMONT ELEMENTARY, TEACHER'S LOUNGE - SAME TIME
Three teachers stand in a huddle drinking coffee and
scrolling through phones. MR. MARCUM, a 40 year old
teacher....
MR. MARCUM
Found it.
(reading from phone)
'Hello all'...'reminder from inservice
meeting'...'bullet points'...Ahh.
'Social Media use. 'It is best to make
all personal social media accounts
private and not friend or follow
students or parents.'
SARAH WILLIAMSON a 45 year old teacher with a phony smile on
her face adds...
SARAH
Oh and don't forget...
(reading from phone)
'serious consequences will be enforced
for those who choose to use social to
speak negatively on the district.'
MS. TINKAM, 35, rolls her eyes at this.
SARAH (CONT'D)
Feels a little intrusive. Telling us
who we can and can't speak to outside
of work.
MS. TINKAM
It's censorship. She can't just tell
us that.
MR. MARCUM
I think she's just reminding us of the
policy. Not that I agree with it but
it's probably not her stance.
MS. TINKAM
I don't agree with it either.
Sarah nods in agreement with this view. Mr. Marcum changes
his tune.
MR. MARCUM
Yea. Come to think of it, it probably
is her stance!

2A 3.
SARAH
She probably has no life on social
media.
MR. MARCUM
True. Because it kind of requires her
to have something interesting to
share.
He waits for a laugh or sign of agreement but it doesn't
come. He's not the most popular amongst this group.
MS. TINKAM
I checked. Her's is private but it
probably always has been.
SARAH
It's not gonna stop the anonymous
twitter page anyway. That's the one
doing the most damage.
MS. TINKAM
Yes.
(cautiously)
But it's needed. People should be
getting a window into what's going on
inside the district.
Small smiles begin to appear.
MS. TINKAM (CONT'D)
I mean if the district doesn't want
people to know what's going on, all
the more reason to question.
SARAH
Can't say I disagree.
The bell rings. They disperse.
INT. FREEMONT ELEMENTARY, MEETING ROOM - SAME TIME
Principal Reeves and JANICE NEELY, a 31 year old faculty
member, set up name cards, writing utensils and reading
materials.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Your son, Jay?
(off Janice's nod)
Yes. Jay. He's so lucky to have a mom
in special education. How long ago was

he diagnosed?
JANICE
He's 13. So he was... 3 when he was
diagnosed. Asperger's is more
identifiable. Luckily, despite not so
great insurance, we had a wonderful
doctor. I was in school and working
two jobs at the time. Ya know before I
became a paraprofessional and started
making the big bucks.
They laugh at her sarcasm.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
I'm glad to hear that too. How's he
doing now?
JANICE
Not much has changed in terms of
finances but being able to work here,
getting the special employee transfer,
and having him just down the road for
middle school is a huge help.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Lincoln Middle is a great school. I've
known Principal Lowe for a long time.
She's a great leader. He'll get what
he needs there.
JANICE
The school he's actually zoned for is
twenty miles from me. No judgements. I
know running a school isn't easy but
it's low performing and the
environment...I'm just grateful. I
wouldn't be able to afford getting him
there and back every day.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Thanks for your help setting this all
up. Your attitude and energy is so
great to have around. Especially when
it comes to your commitment to SLD
students.
JANICE
That's kind of you to say. Having a
child like Jay taught me to keep a
level head.

PRINCIPAL REEVES
Well I'm gonna try and adopt your
attitude more often.
JANICE
Believe me, it didn't come naturally.
Sometimes at home and even at school
he lashes out. But patience is the
most invaluable thing I learned
training for this work.
They smile at the sentiment as they continue setting up.
3 OMIT
4 EXT. FREEMONT ELEMENTARY, PICNIC TABLES - CONTINUOUS
Principal Reeves smiles and carries two capri-sun pouches in
her hands as she makes her way to PRINCIPAL LOWE, 40s, of
Lincoln Middle school. Lowe is a kind-hearted individual who
has an obvious rapport with Principal Reeves.
She takes a seat across from Lowe and hands her a drink.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Sorry for making you wait. Your ears
must have been ringing. One of my
parapros was just talking about how
lucky she is to have her son in your
care.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
Jay's mom?
Principal Reeves nods.
PRINCIPAL LOWE (CONT'D)
Glad to hear she feels that way. So,
what's up? I got your message to call,
but whatever you wanted to talk about
sounded like we should meet in person.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
(sighing)
It's this social media thing. I'm
exhausted by it. I held a teacher
meeting during the in-service, where
they threw the most elaborate and
difficult scenarios at me.
Principal Lowe laughs.

PRINCIPAL REEVES (CONT'D)
And after I wiped the sweat from my
forehead, I advised having private
profiles and not using socials to
communicate with parents.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
UmHmm. A pretty obvious solution.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
But everyday it's something else. So
and so thought a teacher's Facebook
status could be misconstrued. Someone
else's Instagram is still public.
Another person is Facebook friends
with EVERY parent in their class.
More laughter.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
Sounds about right.
Principal Reeves rubs her temples.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
(defeated)
Kinda needed a little assurance that
I'm doing enough.
(re-energized)
But also. The anonymous twitter
account that keeps starting all the
twitter wars.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
(unthreatened)
Real ACS District?
PRINCIPAL REEVES
(nodding)
It's like they googled 'most
pretentious Twitter handle.'
They share a laugh. Principal Reeves plays with the straw of
her drink.
PRINCIPAL REEVES (CONT'D)
Did the district contact you about it?
PRINCIPAL LOWE
Me personally? No. You?

PRINCIPAL REEVES
Yes. Everyone knows it's a district
employee...and maybe it's just my
paranoia about not getting through to
my faculty but it seems like the
district suspects one of mine.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
Do think it is? you
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Honestly? No. So far I've only had
small infractions come from my
faculty. I can't think of anyone in my
building with that kind of attitude
toward the district.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
Well then, just keep doing what you're
doing. Everyone's keeping an eye out
for this person. It will come to light
sooner or later.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Ugh. You're right. Patience.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
You got it. You're doing a good job...
In fact, I need advice. your
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Shoot.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
I've got a student who's claiming to
be bullied. The parent is convinced.
And their child seems like an unlikely
instigator but to my knowledge they're
actually the one doing the bullying.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
And you're not sure if it will go over
well, accusing the unlikely suspect of
being the real bully?
PRINCIPAL LOWE
Exactly.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
You're a great leader. The kind we all
look up to. If you're seeing it, even

if it looks unfamiliar, you've gotta
trust your gut...
Principal Reeves sees Ben with an urgent look in one of the
school's doorways.
PRINCIPAL REEVES (CONT'D)
Sorry to do this to you but I gotta
run.
PRINCIPAL LOWE
I appreciate the pep talk. Let me know
how things go. You got this.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Will do and you do the same.
Principal Reeves stands up and waves to Lowe. She takes a
deep breath and heads back into the school.
5 INT. FREEMONT ELEMENTARY, HALLWAY - DAY
A new day. Principal Reeves heads down a hallway. Sarah
Williamson makes her way toward Reeves, head down in her
phone.
CRASH! Sarah walks right into Principal Reeves. Principal
Reeves kindly grabs Sarah's arms to keep her from falling.
Sarah looks up. Her brows are furrowed as she realizes what
is happening. She quickly pastes a smile on.
SARAH
Oh gosh. I'm so sorry.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
It's alright. Are you okay, Sarah?
SARAH
I'm fine. Thank you.
Sarah continues down the hall. The interaction leaves an odd
feeling in Principal Reeves' gut. She continues, reaches a
perpendicular hallway, hears a commotion.
She peeks around the corner to find Janice Neely speaking
with her teacher.
ANGLE ON:
TEACHER, a 50 year old man who Janice assists, comforts her
as she starts to tear up. She's got her bag and jacket on.

TEACHER
Hey it's okay.
JANICE
I'm so sorry. His principal said I
have to come get him. I can't believe
she would suspend him for the fight.
Jay wouldn't fight. The kid he
supposedly fought has been bullying
him for weeks. She does nothing and
now wants to suspend my son?
Janice is overwhelmed in this moment.
Teacher gently strokes Janice's arm.
JANICE (CONT'D)
I'm so sorry to do this to you but I
need to pick him up now.
TEACHER
It's okay. I promise. The day is
almost over. You go take care of your
son.
JANICE
Thank you so much. Thank you.
She hurries away and Teacher goes back in his classroom.
ANGLE ON:
Principal Reeves is shocked by what she's just heard. BUZZ!
She checks her phone. On her screen, a tweet reads...
'THE DISTRICT IS CHANGING THE TESTING POLICY AGAIN. WHAT A
TRAIN WRECK. LEADERSHIP IS SUCH A FAILURE. TERMINATE THEM
ALL.'
Her eyes shift up to read the Twitter handle. On her screen
it reads...
'@MRS.SARAH.WILLIAMS'
Her mind is racing. CLICK! She takes a screenshot.
She taps Sarah's handle. Now on her page the tweet is gone.
She opens her texts, drafts a message. On her screen it
reads...

TO ANGELA LOWE - 'THEY'RE GETTING BOLDER.'
BUZZ! Still on her screen, before she can hit send another
Twitter notification appears. It reads...
'THE DISTRICT IS CHANGING THE TESTING POLICY AGAIN. WHAT A
TRAIN WRECK. LEADERSHIP IS SUCH A FAILURE. TERMINATE THEM
ALL.'
Her eyes shift up again to read the Twitter handle. This time
on the screen it reads...
'@ANONYMOUSDISTRICT.'
Her eyes light up. Got 'em!
6 INT. SUPERINTENDENT'S OFFICE, WAITING ROOM - DAY
Inside of a mostly empty waiting area a SECRETARY works at a
desk. Principal Reeves enters.
SECRETARY
Hello. How can I help you?
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Hi. I have an appointment with the
Superintendent in five.
SECRETARY
Reeves?
(off her nod)
You can have a seat. He'll call you in
shortly.
PRINCIPAL REEVES
Thank you.
Principal Reeves takes a seat. She uses the silent moment to
collect her thoughts. BUZZ! She opens her phone. On the
screen is a text that reads...
'FROM ANGELA LOWE - THOUGHT YOU MIGHT BE INTERESTED IN THIS.'
An ellipsis hovers before a photo appears.
Principal Reeves sets her phone down. She tries to clear her
head but is drawn back. She picks up her phone. On the screen
it shows...
A screenshot of a Facebook status from Janice Neely. The
profile photo is she and her son. It reads...

'TODAY I GET A CALL FROM MY SON'S PRINCIPAL, PRINCIPAL LOWE
SAYING THAT SHE'S SUSPENDED MY SON FOR DEFENDING HIMSELF FROM
A KNOWN BULLY. TURNS OUT LINCOLN MIDDLE AND PRINCIPAL LOWE
LOOK THE OTHER WAY ON BULLYING.'
Principal Reeves reads it in disbelief. This is not the
Janice I know.
She looks back to her screen, focused on...
'74 COMMENTS'
A sudden onslaught of anxiety hits her when the
Superintendent's door opens...
SUPERINTENDENT
Principal Reeves. I'm ready for you.
Principal Reeves is left conflicted.
FADE OUT.
