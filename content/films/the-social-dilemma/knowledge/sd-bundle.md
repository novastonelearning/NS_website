Social Dilemma
Principal Reeves grapples with a growing social media dilemma as she stands in her
office. After a tense phone call with Ben, her administrative assistant, she learns that an
anonymous Twitter account has been posting critical comments about the school district. The
situation escalates when she overhears teachers in the lounge discussing a recent in-service
meeting that reinforced the need for strict social media guidelines. The teachers express their
frustrations, perceiving the policies as intrusive censorship.
As Principal Reeves reflects on the atmosphere at Fremont Elementary, she recalls a
conversation with Janice Neely, a paraprofessional who has a personal connection to the school.
They discuss the challenges of supporting students with special needs, especially in light of
Janice's son, Jay, who has Asperger's. This conversation highlights the dedication of staff
members like Janice, who strive to advocate for their students despite the overwhelming
pressures of the educational system.
The situation becomes more complex as Principal Reeves meets with Principal Lowe
from Lincoln Middle School. During their conversation, Reeves shares her frustrations about
managing social media communications and the anxiety surrounding the anonymous Twitter
account that seems to have insider knowledge about district happenings. Despite these
challenges, Principal Lowe reassures Reeves that she is doing well in her role and emphasizes
the importance of patience in navigating the district’s social dynamics.
The tension heightens when a teacher, Sarah Williamson, nearly collides with Principal
Reeves while distracted by her phone. This encounter leaves Reeves with an unsettling feeling,
prompting her to investigate further. As she walks down the hallway, she overhears a distressed
Janice speaking with a teacher about her son’s suspension for defending himself against a known
bully. This revelation strikes a chord with Reeves, as she understands the significant implications
of such incidents on students and their families.
When Principal Reeves checks her phone, she is shocked to find a tweet from Sarah's
account criticizing the district's leadership and policy changes. In disbelief, she captures a
screenshot, recognizing the need to address this issue head-on. The situation escalates when she
discovers another critical tweet from an anonymous account, solidifying her suspicion that the
discontent within the district is manifesting through social media.
As Principal Reeves enters the superintendent's office for a scheduled meeting, she
receives a troubling text from Principal Lowe, which includes a screenshot of a Facebook post by
Janice. The post accuses Principal Lowe of failing to address bullying at Lincoln Middle School,
leaving Reeves conflicted about how to respond to these unfolding events. The implications of
social media on school leadership and community perception weigh heavily on her mind as she
prepares for her meeting.
